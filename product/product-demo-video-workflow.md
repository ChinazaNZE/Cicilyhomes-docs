CicilyHomes Product Demo Video – Documented Workflow
Video Title: Product Demo (Landing Page Focus)
Duration: ≤ 90 seconds
Tool used for recording: Windows Snipping Tool
Editing tool: Canva Business
Date created: 28 October 2026
1. Objective
Create a short product demonstration video that shows the real live website (https://cicilyhomes.com) working, focusing on the public landing page and key customer-facing features.
The video must stay under 90 seconds and clearly demonstrate the product is functional.
2. Process Followed
Stage
Step
Details
1. Planning
Script development
Created a timed 85-second script focused only on the landing page (hero section, benefits, search, How it works, Why tenants choose us). No landlord listing/onboarding included.


Rehearsal
Practised the script while navigating the live website to ensure natural flow and correct timing.
2. Recording
Tool
Windows Snipping Tool (Win + Shift + R)


Setup
Clean browser window on cicilyhomes.com, microphone enabled, address bar visible.


Method
Recorded screen + voiceover simultaneously while clicking through the homepage features.


Output
MP4 file saved in Videos → Screen Recordings
3. Editing
Tool
Canva Business


Actions
- Uploaded the raw recording<br>- Trimmed to final length<br>- Added text overlays and captions<br>- Applied brand colours and logo<br>- Added subtle background music<br>- Exported final MP4 (1080p)
4. Review & Delivery
Internal review
Shared with team for feedback


Final versions

3. Key Principles Applied
Showed the real product (live website), not mockups
Kept the video focused on one clear goal (Product Demo)
Maintained transparent and professional tone
Ensured the video is short, clear, and easy to understand
